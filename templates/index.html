Understanding Climate Change in the Pacific Islands and Territories

Pacific Islands & Territories are uniquely vulnerable to the impacts of climate change. From rising sea surface temperatures disrupting local marine ecosystems to shifting weather patterns threatening agricultural yields, the consequences are multi-faceted. This application transforms open data into a clear climate change narrative across {{ territory_count }} Pacific countries and territories.

How It Works

Selection

Select a Pacific Island or Territory to establish geographic and demographic context before diving into the data.

Themes

The visualizations are organized into three pillars — Land & Food, Ocean & Atmosphere, and People & Economy.

Comparison

Add a second country or territory to any story to see how two places' trends actually differ.
