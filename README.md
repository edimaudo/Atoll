# Understanding Climate Change in the Pacific Islands and Territories

Pacific Islands & Territories are uniquely vulnerable to the impacts of climate change. From rising sea surface temperatures disrupting local marine ecosystems to shifting weather patterns threatening agricultural yields, the consequences are multi-faceted. 
This application transforms open data into a clear climate change narrative across 22 Pacific countries and territories.

## Design
**Frontend**: Jinja Templates, Plotly, Pandas, CSS

**Backend**: FastAPI, Vercel
